### Signature Methodological Features (by segment)

#### Kinder & Kids — True Bilingual Immersion

- The **only school in Guarapuava** with a genuinely bilingual methodology — not bilingual "with elements" like competitors, but full bilingual immersion from day one
- Dedicated **Kinder Center** — an age-appropriate, fully immersive English environment designed exclusively for children ages 4–5
- Children develop **natural fluency through play, storytelling with values, and cultural routines** — zero translation dependency, zero grammar worksheets
- **Star Program spaces** (all conducted in English): Cinema, Cooking, Sports, VR, Studio, Learning Center — experiences unavailable at any other school in Guarapuava
- Cultural events physically immerse children in American traditions: *Morning at Disney, Halloween, Thanksgiving, St. Patrick's Day, Easter, Kid's Fair*
- CEFR-tracked progress from earliest levels — building the foundation for the exclusive **American Dual Diploma** pathway
- Small class sizes, specialist teachers, quantified and monitored progress per unit
- **Safety infrastructure**: gated entry, 2 security guards, Hall team, 100% recorded environment — built for parental confidence

#### Teens — Global Formation + Bilingual Depth

- Bilingual methodology combined with **global citizenship formation** — the unique Teens differentiator
- **Global Day / Global Knowledge** — exclusive recurring program where students engage with real international topics, world events, and global challenges entirely in English. Students debate, research, present, and collaborate — building a genuine global perspective alongside language fluency. No other school in Guarapuava has an equivalent.
- **International Topics & Cultural Weeks** — recurring thematic immersions in world cultures, current events, and global trends; students build the mindset and vocabulary to participate in real international conversations
- **Perspectives** — students write, direct, and produce original short films, premiered at Cine XV in a full Oscar-style ceremony for families and guests; the most ambitious student production event in the city
- Star Program immersion: Conversation Club, Cinema, VR, Studio, Sports, Cooking, Learning Center
- Access to **exchange programs** and **American university courses** before graduating high school
- **Grow Global** integrated track: robotics + coding + entrepreneurship + leadership, 100% in English

#### Adults (Empower & Advanced) — Living It + Immersive Communication

- **Living It Classes** — the signature CCBEU adult methodology. Instead of grammar drills, students *live* the English language through simulations of real-life situations: travelling abroad, shopping at an American store, job interviews, business meetings, ordering at restaurants, navigating airports — as if they were physically in the United States
- The methodology is called **Immersive Communication**: you learn by speaking, experiencing, and doing — not memorizing rules
- Classes use **Cambridge-certified top-tier English books** combined with in-room technology: native speaker audio and video, real-world contexts, interactive exercises
- **Star Program as speed multiplier**: students who want to evolve up to 3x faster add Conversation Club, Cinema, VR, Cooking, and Learning Center to their weekly routine. This is offered *after* the core 2-hour sell — not upfront, to avoid overwhelming prospects
- **C1 CEFR Guaranteed** — if a student completes the full program without reaching C1 (advanced proficiency), they continue studying for free. No competitor in Guarapuava offers a performance guarantee of any kind.
- **Make-up classes always available** — no student ever permanently loses a class due to absence
- **AI Teacher on WhatsApp, 24/7** — practice real conversations, ask grammar questions, keep English active between classes
- Specific adult tracks: Business English, travel English, professional certification prep (TOEFL, IELTS), Conversation Classes for graduates

## 6. TARGET AUDIENCES (Personas)

### Persona 1 — "Pais do Kinder/Kids" (Parents, 28–45)

- Investing in the critical window: early childhood is the optimal phase for language acquisition
- Motivated by: genuine bilingualism, natural fluency, safety, American Dual Diploma pathway, world readiness
- Pain: unsure how to distinguish real immersion from schools that just have "English activities"
- Fear trigger: *"Perder a melhor fase da vida para ser fluente."* The brain is primed for language now — waiting is a permanent cost.
- Message hook: *"Só no CCBEU seu filho aprende com a única metodologia imersiva bilíngue da cidade, em 7 salas temáticas — Cinema, VR, Studio, Sports, Cooking, Learning Center e Kinder Center."*

### Persona 2 — "Pais do Teens" (Parents, 35–50)

- Aware their teenager needs more than classroom English to compete globally and enter university/market
- Motivated by: exchange programs, international university access, global perspective, career readiness
- Pain: teen is losing motivation with traditional methods; needs something that excites and challenges
- Message hook: *"Só no CCBEU ele desenvolve uma visão global — com Global Day, International Topics e Cultural Weeks — e o nível de inglês mais alto da cidade."*

### Persona 3 — "Jovem Adulto Empower/Advanced" (18–30)

- Seeking professional growth, salary increase, travel, remote work opportunities with international companies
- Pain: tried apps or cheap online courses, gave up; feels English is hard or "not for them"
- Key stats to deploy: only 5% of Brazilians speak English; only 1% is fluent; bilingual professionals earn up to 61% more; 67%+ of internet content is in English; AI is accelerating everything — English is the access language
- Message hook: *"Com as Living It Classes, você não estuda inglês — você vive. Simulações de viagens, reuniões, situações reais. Como se estivesse nos EUA."*

### Persona 4 — "Grow Global Parent" (Parents, 35–50)

- Acutely aware of AI disruption; worried their child will be left behind in a world of automation
- Wants creativity + leadership + English as a combined package for future-proofing
- Message hook: *"A escola tradicional mata a criatividade. O Grow Global não é só tecnologia — é comportamento e mentalidade. É seu filho protagonista do futuro."*

### Persona 5 — "GoExplore Online Adult" (National, 25–45)

- Lives outside Guarapuava or has a schedule that prevents in-person attendance
- Wants flexibility + real results + community and accountability
- Pain: both self-paced courses (no accountability) and traditional in-person (too rigid) have failed them before
- USP: live weekly classes + AI Teacher 24/7 + class recordings + 500+ video app + flashcards + ebooks — all for less than the combined cost of those separately
